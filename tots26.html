Info

The banners on the cards are the playstyles. Silver is base playstyle and Gold is Playstyle+

sniper: player ability to strike a ball from a distance on target



Brick wall: player's ability to make tackles



bruiser: player's ability to block players using his body



incisive pass: player ability to make through balls



sweeper keeper: goalkeeper's ability to charge at an attacker and stop an attack



wall bounce: player ability to execute wall bounces to beat his man or get an angle for a shot or pass



tiki taka: player ability to have the decision making and composure to make short passes, tiki taka+ would be doing that but with first time passes
